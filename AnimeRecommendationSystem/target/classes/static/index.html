How the Recommendation Algorithm Works

1. What is Content-Based Filtering?

Content-based filtering recommends items similar to what a user liked in the past by analyzing item features (content descriptors). In this project, each anime's content is defined by its genre set and its rating score.

2. Mathematical Formulation: Jaccard Similarity

To measure how similar two anime \(A\) and \(B\) are, we calculate the Jaccard Similarity Coefficient across their genre sets:

$$J(A, B) = \frac{|A \cap B|}{|A \cup B|} = \frac{\text{Shared Genres Count}}{\text{Total Unique Combined Genres}}$$
  • Intersection (\(A \cap B\)): Genres that both anime share.
  • Union (\(A \cup B\)): All unique genres across both anime.
  • Range: Value between 0.0 (no overlap) and 1.0 (identical genres).

3. Quality Weighting (Genre + Rating Hybrid Score)

To ensure that highly rated masterpieces get a slight positive boost among genre-matching candidates, we combine genre similarity with normalized rating:

$$\text{Final Score} = (0.85 \times Jaccard) + \left(0.15 \times \frac{\text{Rating}}{10}\right)$$

Recommendations are then sorted descending by Final Score, and the top \(N\) best matches are presented with an explanation of why they matched!
